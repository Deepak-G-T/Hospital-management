Common contact questions

Find immediate answers to our most frequent patient inquiries.

What are reception and visiting hours?

Main hospital reception and outpatient desks are open Monday through Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Urgent emergency intake is staffed around the clock, 24 hours a day.

How quickly will I receive a reply to my message?

Our patient care coordination team reviews inquiries continuously and typically responds within one business day via email or phone.

Where is patient parking located?

Visitor and patient parking is located directly in front of the main entrance at 120 Health Avenue, with marked accessible parking bays near the reception doors.

Can I reschedule an appointment here?

For fastest service, please log in to your patient account or call reception directly at +1 555 0100 so our staff can check real-time doctor availability with you.
